Games can be an incredible tool for learning and development in young children.
But the modern world is full of the wrong type of games.
99% of "games" released today are glorified slot machines.

Here are some that are great for young kids with developing brains.
None of these are explicitely "educational" or "learning" games.
They're just great games that happen to have a ton of educational value.
